Payment questions

How does the free sample work?

You send up to 100 records from your own list. I trace them with the same process as paid work, so you can judge the quality first.

Can I pay through Upwork or Fiverr?

Yes. If you prefer escrow, we can run the project through either platform. Otherwise payment terms are agreed before the first paid batch.

What counts as a match?

A record where I find at least one phone number or email for the owner. If nothing is found, the record is clearly marked "no result" and you are not charged for it.

Is there a minimum order?

Paid batches start at $25. The free 100-record sample has no minimum.

Do I see the price before work starts?

Always. You get a written quote with volume, fields and turnaround before I start any paid batch.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Start with the free sample and a small paid batch. Monthly plans can be cancelled with one month's notice.
