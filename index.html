FAQ

Before we get started.

What is skip tracing?

Skip tracing finds current contact details, such as phone numbers, emails and mailing addresses, for property owners on your list, so you can reach them directly.

Do you guarantee a phone number for every owner?

No. No skip tracing provider can. Records with no usable result are flagged clearly, so you can see exactly what was found and what was not.

How does the free sample work?

You send up to 100 records from your own list. I trace them with the same process as paid work, so you can judge quality before committing.

How do you price your services?

Skip tracing is priced per record, with discounts for larger batches. Ongoing VA support is a monthly fee. See the pricing page for starting rates.

What file format will I receive?

CSV or Excel, shaped to your CRM or dialer template where possible. Google Sheets works too.

Is my property list kept confidential?

Yes. Your lists are used only for your project and are never resold or shared. Read the privacy policy.

Do you work US hours?

I am in Pakistan Standard Time and arrange overlap with US mornings or evenings for calls and handover, depending on your timezone.

Are the portfolio samples from actual clients?

No. The tables on this page use fictional demonstration data and are not represented as client work. Real client data is never published.
