Last updated: November 23, 2026. This policy explains how Ali Hasnain VA Services handles information from this website and from client projects.

Information from this website

If you send an inquiry, I receive your name, email address, selected service and the project details you write. I use this only to reply to you and to prepare quotes. The site does not use advertising trackers.

Client lists and project data

Lists you send for skip tracing or research are used only to complete your project. I do not resell, share or reuse client lists for any other client or purpose. Please do not paste personal data into the website form; I will share a secure way to send files after we speak.

Retention and deletion

After a project ends, I keep deliverables for a short period in case of follow-up questions, then delete client files. You can ask me to delete your files and inquiry details at any time.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for using delivered contact data in line with the laws that apply to you, including Do Not Call, texting and anti-spam rules.

Third parties

Messages may pass through email and messaging providers such as Gmail or WhatsApp. Links to LinkedIn, Upwork, Fiverr, Facebook and Instagram go to sites with their own policies.
